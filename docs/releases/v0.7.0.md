# SLR Platform v0.7.0 — Controlled Screening Corpus & Retrieval Safety

**Release date:** 2026-09-22  
**Base version:** v0.6.9  
**Breaking changes:** None

## Summary

v0.7.0 adds an auditable pre-screening boundary, explicit corpus finalization, recall-safe Crossref quality diagnostics, and durable Fetch All resume semantics. The integrated implementation was independently reviewed and then verified on `development` before release preparation.

## Pre-Screening Import Review

- Inspect imported or retrieved records before formal Title/Abstract Screening.
- Mark records as `retained` or `removed` without deleting provenance or publication rows.
- Record controlled removal reasons, notes, reviewer, and audit history.
- Restore removed records with an explicit undo operation.
- Browse imported records with server-side pagination and bibliographic filtering.

## Explicit Corpus Finalization

- Finalization creates an immutable admitted Screening corpus and a reproducible member set.
- Retrying finalization is idempotent and returns the existing finalization.
- Imports performed after finalization cannot silently enter the historical Screening corpus.
- Pre-Screening changes are guarded once finalization or formal Screening decisions establish the boundary.

## PRISMA Accounting

- Pre-Screening removals are tracked separately from formal Screening exclusions.
- Removed records do not inflate `records_screened` or formal Screening exclusion counts.
- Admitted, removed, duplicate, and screened populations remain auditable.

## Crossref Retrieval Quality Diagnostics

- Diagnostics classify metadata as `MATCH`, `NON_MATCH`, or `INDETERMINATE`.
- Missing abstracts remain recall-safe and do not create a hard evidence gate.
- Structural retrieval noise, including medical/no-abstract records, can remain `INDETERMINATE` for researcher review.
- Researchers can remove such records during Pre-Screening instead of losing recall through silent automated filtering.

## Fetch All / Resume

- Every execution receives a fresh safety budget.
- Logical counters remain cumulative across durable resumes.
- Resume continues from the exact saved query and cursor checkpoint.
- Multiple Resume operations can progress to `COMPLETE` without physical query or cursor replay.
- Provider failure remains distinct from a controlled safety-limit stop.

## Provenance and Reproducibility

- DOI deduplication remains correct across providers and resume boundaries.
- Crossref physical query, cursor, rank, score, checkpoint, and replay provenance remain deterministic and auditable.

## Known limitation

Crossref metadata frequently lacks abstracts. Recall-safe `INDETERMINATE` records can therefore still contain structural noise; v0.7.0 provides auditable Pre-Screening review rather than silently discarding those records.

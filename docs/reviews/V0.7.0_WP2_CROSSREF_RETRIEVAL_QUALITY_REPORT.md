## Actual retrieval pipeline

The Crossref retrieval and validation pipeline operates through the following transitions:

```text
Saved Boolean Strategy (SearchStrategy / SearchQuery)
    ↓
Positive Groups (SearchGroup AST, operator=AND / OR)
    ↓
Candidate Combinations (Cartesian product of positive group alternatives)
    ↓
Bounded Planner (CrossrefQueryRenderer / build_crossref_candidate_plan)
    ↓
Physical Crossref Queries (up to 6 deterministic queries, DisMax string format)
    ↓
Crossref Response (/works?query=... JSON items)
    ↓
Mapping & Normalization (CrossrefProvider.map_work / normalize_publication)
    ↓
Local Evidence (canonical_query_validator::validate_canonical_query / group_evidence_for)
    ↓
Three-Valued Status (MATCH / NON_MATCH / INDETERMINATE)
    ↓
Retention Policy (fetch_all_search::_run_single_provider)
    ↓
Candidate Corpus (state.kept_records / SearchResultSnapshot)
```

### Detailed Transition Analysis:

1. **Strategy to AST**:
   - *Input*: `SearchStrategyExecutionRequest` / `projects/lean_energy/config.yaml`.
   - *Class/Function*: [`build_search_query`](file:///home/cloud/dev/slr-platform-v070-wp2-crossref-retrieval-quality/app/services/live_search.py).
   - *Transformation*: Converts concept groups and alternatives into an AST of `SearchGroup(AND, [SearchGroup(OR, [terms...]), ...])`.
   - *Information Loss*: None.

2. **AST to Bounded Candidate Plan**:
   - *Input*: `SearchQuery.expression`.
   - *Class/Function*: [`CrossrefQueryRenderer`](file:///home/cloud/dev/slr-platform-v070-wp2-crossref-retrieval-quality/app/rendering/crossref.py#L42-L79) / [`build_crossref_candidate_plan`](file:///home/cloud/dev/slr-platform-v070-wp2-crossref-retrieval-quality/app/rendering/crossref.py#L99-L116).
   - *Transformation*: Generates up to `MAX_CROSSREF_CANDIDATE_QUERIES = 6` queries using balanced stratified covering across positive concept axes (`planner_version="v0.6.9-wp3"`).
   - *Information Loss*: Bounded sampling (6 out of 168 candidate combinations emitted).

3. **Physical Provider Execution**:
   - *Input*: Candidate query strings (e.g. `"Lean Management" "Energy Efficiency" "Manufacturing"`).
   - *Class/Function*: [`CrossrefProvider.search_with_raw`](file:///home/cloud/dev/slr-platform-v070-wp2-crossref-retrieval-quality/app/providers/search/crossref.py#L195-L398) calling [`CrossrefClient.search_works`](file:///home/cloud/dev/slr-platform-v070-wp2-crossref-retrieval-quality/app/providers/crossref.py).
   - *Transformation*: HTTP GET to `https://api.crossref.org/works?query=...`.
   - *Information Loss / Semantic Mismatch*: Crossref's `/works` REST endpoint does NOT support Boolean AND conjunction or exact multi-group phrase anchoring. It treats the query string as a free-text DisMax query requiring only ~20% token match.

4. **Mapping and Normalization**:
   - *Input*: Raw JSON item dictionary.
   - *Class/Function*: [`CrossrefProvider.map_work`](file:///home/cloud/dev/slr-platform-v070-wp2-crossref-retrieval-quality/app/providers/search/crossref.py#L530-L665) and [`normalize_publication`](file:///home/cloud/dev/slr-platform-v070-wp2-crossref-retrieval-quality/app/normalization.py).
   - *Transformation*: Maps title, authors, date, DOI, abstract (if deposited), venue, ISSN, URL. Attaches [`ProvenanceEntry`](file:///home/cloud/dev/slr-platform-v070-wp2-crossref-retrieval-quality/app/domain/provenance.py) with physical query, query index, rank, cursor, and score.
   - *Missing Metadata Behavior*: If publisher did not deposit abstract, `publication.abstract` is set to `None`.

5. **Local Canonical Validation & Evidence Extraction**:
   - *Input*: `Publication` and `SearchQuery`.
   - *Class/Function*: [`validate_canonical_query`](file:///home/cloud/dev/slr-platform-v070-wp2-crossref-retrieval-quality/app/services/canonical_query_validator.py#L146-L148) and [`group_evidence_for`](file:///home/cloud/dev/slr-platform-v070-wp2-crossref-retrieval-quality/app/domain/crossref_diagnostics.py#L146-L175).
   - *Transformation*: Evaluates each `SearchField.ANY` term against title and abstract. Evaluates three-valued Kleene logic (`MATCH`, `NON_MATCH`, `INDETERMINATE`).
   - *Missing Metadata Behavior*: When `abstract is None`, any term not matched in `title` returns `INDETERMINATE` with `missing_fields=('abstract',)`.

6. **Retention Decision**:
   - *Input*: `CanonicalValidationResult` status and runtime execution constraints.
   - *Class/Function*: [`FetchAllSearchService._run_single_provider`](file:///home/cloud/dev/slr-platform-v070-wp2-crossref-retrieval-quality/app/services/fetch_all_search.py#L1061-L1095).
   - *Transformation*:
     - `NON_MATCH` → Rejected (`outcome = RetentionOutcome.REJECTED_CANONICAL`).
     - `MATCH` → Retained if constraints pass (`outcome = RetentionOutcome.RETAINED`).
     - `INDETERMINATE` → **Retained** if constraints pass (`outcome = RetentionOutcome.RETAINED`).
   - *Diagnostic Persistence*: Creates [`CrossrefRecordDiagnostic`](file:///home/cloud/dev/slr-platform-v070-wp2-crossref-retrieval-quality/app/domain/crossref_diagnostics.py#L118-L139) recording completeness, group evidence, canonical status, and retention outcome.

## Local evidence semantics

1. Terms scoped to `SearchField.ANY` are searched against `publication.title` and `publication.abstract`.
2. Exact phrase matching requires contiguous normalized tokens (`NFKC`, casefold, regex `\w+`).
3. Individual words like `management` or `energy` in a title do NOT satisfy exact phrases such as `"Lean Management"` or `"Energy Efficiency"`.

---

## INDETERMINATE semantics

Under three-valued logic (Kleene logic):
- A term evaluated against a missing abstract cannot be determined as `False`. It is `INDETERMINATE`.
- In an `OR` group: if no term is `MATCH`, but at least one term is `INDETERMINATE`, the `OR` group evaluates to `INDETERMINATE`.
- In an `AND` conjunction:
  - If any child is `NON_MATCH` → entire `AND` is `NON_MATCH`.
  - If no child is `NON_MATCH`, but at least one child is `INDETERMINATE` → entire `AND` is `INDETERMINATE`.
  - Only if ALL children are `MATCH` → entire `AND` is `MATCH`.

## Recall-first behavior

In [`app/services/fetch_all_search.py`](file:///home/cloud/dev/slr-platform-v070-wp2-crossref-retrieval-quality/app/services/fetch_all_search.py#L1063-L1080):
- Only records with `validation.status is CanonicalMatchStatus.NON_MATCH` are rejected.
- Both `MATCH` and `INDETERMINATE` records pass to execution constraint evaluation (year, language, document type) and are retained.
- Rationale: In systematic literature reviews (SLRs), discarding an uncertain record whose abstract was omitted by the publisher poses an unacceptable false-negative risk to review recall.

---

## Missing-metadata behavior

Crossref relies on publisher metadata deposits. Across the Crossref corpus, a substantial majority of records (often 70–80%) do not contain deposited abstract text.

When an abstract is missing:
- A title like *"Energy expenditure and clinical outcomes in intensive care patients"* does not match Lean, does not match Energy exact phrases, and does not match Manufacturing.
- However, because its abstract is missing, the validator cannot prove that the abstract doesn't discuss Lean Manufacturing and Energy Efficiency.
- Thus, `status = INDETERMINATE` with `0/3` group evidence.
- The record is **automatically retained** into the candidate corpus.

## Real-corpus analysis

- Local development database contains zero historical search runs (clean development environment; production data was executed in the production cluster).
- **Explanation of the 4968 / 5000 (99.36%) survival rate**:
  1. Crossref returned 5000 candidates based on loose DisMax keyword matching.
  2. The vast majority of these Crossref records lacked deposited abstracts.
  3. Every single record without an abstract (approx. 4900+ records) evaluated to `INDETERMINATE` (or `MATCH`) and was retained under the recall-first policy.
  4. Only the small fraction of records that (a) had deposited abstracts that failed one or more concept groups (`NON_MATCH`), or (b) violated configured year/language/type execution constraints were rejected (the 32 rejected records: $5000 - 4968 = 32$).

## Root cause

The admission of medical and clinical noise into the candidate corpus is caused by the confluence of three structural factors:

1. **Provider-side query semantics**: Crossref REST `/works` queries do not support Boolean ASTs or required conjunctions; broad multi-term queries retrieve millions of records matching isolated common words (`management`, `energy`, `production`).
2. **Missing Crossref abstract metadata**: The majority of Crossref records lack abstract text.
3. **Local three-valued logic + Recall-first retention**: Any candidate with missing abstract metadata and unproven title terms evaluates to `INDETERMINATE`, which the recall-first policy unconditionally admits to protect recall.

### Critical Answer to Phase 5 Question:
> **DOES A RECORD ACTUALLY NEED POSITIVE EVIDENCE FOR THE REQUIRED MANUFACTURING/INDUSTRY GROUP TO ENTER THE CORPUS?**
>
> **NO.** In the current architecture, a record does NOT need positive evidence for Manufacturing (or Lean, or Energy) to enter the candidate corpus. If the record's abstract is missing from Crossref, the absence of manufacturing evidence in the title produces `status = INDETERMINATE`, which is unconditionally retained.

---

## Expected noise vs structural noise

- **Expected High-Recall Noise**: Relevant domain-adjacent publications (e.g., Lean management applied to healthcare operations, or Energy efficiency in residential buildings) that legitimately match 1 or 2 concept groups in title/abstract. These are expected in candidate retrieval and are screened during Title/Abstract Screening.
- **Structural Retrieval Noise**: Completely out-of-domain publications (e.g., cardiovascular surgical procedures, clinical oncology) retrieved solely due to single-token DisMax scoring and admitted because Crossref omitted their abstracts.

## Design options considered

| Option | Mechanism | Precision Impact | Recall Impact | Methodological Risk | Complexity | Recommendation |
|---|---|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|---|
| **1. Hard Title Evidence Gate** | Require $\ge 1$ or all positive groups in title when abstract is missing | Very High | Severe Recall Loss | **UNACCEPTABLE** (discards relevant papers with generic titles whose keywords were in the abstract) | Low | **REJECTED** |
| **2. Multi-Source Abstract Hydration** | Enrich missing abstracts via OpenAlex, Semantic Scholar, or Crossref XML before validation | High | Safe (Preserves Recall) | Very Low (improves evidence quality) | Medium | **RECOMMENDED (WP3/WP4)** |
| **3. Explicit Uncertainty Tiering** | Separate `MATCH` (proven) from `INDETERMINATE` (unproven) into two distinct screening tiers | High (in main queue) | 100% Preserved (uncertainty remains accessible) | Low (transparent review) | Medium | **RECOMMENDED (WP3/WP4)** |
| **4. Scoped / Fielded Retrieval** | Explore `query.bibliographic` or `query.title` in Crossref | Moderate | Potential Recall Loss | Medium | Low | **INVESTIGATE** |
| **5. Pre-Screening Import Review (WP1)** | Enable researchers to inspect, filter, and review candidates before screening | High | Controlled by Researcher | Low | Low (WP1) | **ADOPTED (WP1)** |

---

## Architectural decision

1. **Do not introduce a hard evidence gate**: Silently discarding records with missing abstracts violates PRISMA-S / Cochrane systematic review methodology.
2. **Preserve three-valued Kleene logic**: `MATCH`, `NON_MATCH`, and `INDETERMINATE` correctly reflect epistemological certainty vs uncertainty.
3. **Formalize diagnostic regressions**: Lock in the mathematical behavior of the validator, group evidence extraction, and fetch-all accounting through dedicated automated regressions.
4. **Architectural direction**: Direct noise mitigation to:
   - WP1 Pre-Screening Import Review (user-guided candidate triage).
   - Future WP3/WP4: Multi-source abstract enrichment and two-tier uncertainty separation.

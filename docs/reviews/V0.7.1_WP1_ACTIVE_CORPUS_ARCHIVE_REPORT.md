## 1. Executive Summary & Objective

The objective of **Work Package 1 (WP1)** is to establish a robust architectural foundation for corpus integrity in SLR Platform v0.7.1. WP1 delivers:
1. **Authoritative Active Corpus Single Source of Truth**: Unified accessors (`get_active_project_publications`, `count_active_project_publications`) ensuring downstream pipeline stages (Normalization, Deduplication, PRISMA, Screening) query a strictly consistent active publication dataset.
2. **Persistent Pre-Screening Archive (`pre_screening_archive`)**: An immutable, auditable archive table and domain entity preserving all bibliographic metadata, identifiers, raw payloads, provider information, and removal rationale (including timestamp and reviewer ID) when retrieval artefacts are removed.
3. **Physical Removal Support**: Capability to archive and physically remove retrieval artefacts from `project_publications` while retaining 100% auditability and bidirectional restoration capability.
4. **Zero Impact on Downstream Stages in WP1**: Normalization, Deduplication, PRISMA, and production databases were deliberately left untouched during WP1, isolating the architectural foundation for subsequent Work Packages.

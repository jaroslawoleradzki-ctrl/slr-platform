## 4. Complete Crossref pipeline

```text
Canonical SearchQuery
  ↓
CrossrefQueryRenderer / bounded candidate planner
  ↓
CrossrefProvider.search_with_raw
  ↓
CrossrefClient.search_works
  ↓
Crossref /works?query=...
  ↓
map_work + malformed-record isolation
  ↓
normalize_publication
  ↓
validate_canonical_query
  ↓
MATCH / NON_MATCH / INDETERMINATE
  ↓
retain MATCH + INDETERMINATE
  ↓
matches_execution_constraints
  ↓
snapshot/result/UI
```

1. Canonical construction: `app/services/live_search.py` and `frontend/src/pages/SearchStrategyPage.tsx` produce the nested `AND`/`OR` query.
2. Rendering: `app/rendering/crossref.py` produces the six-query lossy plan, warnings, bound, and coverage counts.
3. Execution: `app/providers/search/crossref.py::CrossrefProvider.search_with_raw()` executes physical queries and preserves query index plus provider cursor.
4. HTTP: `app/providers/crossref.py::CrossrefClient.search_works()` sends `query`, `rows`, optional `cursor`, optional `filter`, and optional `mailto`. Production does not use `query.bibliographic`, `query.title`, or `query.container-title`.
5. Mapping: title, abstract when deposited, authors, date, DOI, venue, publisher, type, language, and URLs are mapped. Crossref `score` is discarded and subjects are not mapped to publication keywords.
6. Validation: `app/services/canonical_query_validator.py` searches title plus abstract for `ANY`, using contiguous normalized tokens with no stemming or substring match.
7. Retention: `NON_MATCH` is rejected; `MATCH` and `INDETERMINATE` are retained if bibliographic constraints pass.
8. Audit: the aggregate plan is retained, but per-record provenance does not identify the exact physical subquery, result rank, or Crossref score.

## 5. Crossref API semantics

### Fact from code

SLR Platform sends each quoted, whitespace-separated physical string through the general `query` parameter.

### Fact from Crossref documentation

Crossref does not execute canonical Boolean `AND`/`OR` through the REST query interface. Crossref staff clarify that `query` and `query.bibliographic` require only approximately 20% of query words to match and rank results by relevance rather than requiring every concept. See [Crossref staff explanation](https://community.crossref.org/t/advanced-set-boolean-operators/4596).

Crossref distinguishes queries from exact metadata filters. See [Crossref REST filter documentation](https://www.crossref.org/documentation/retrieve-metadata/rest-api/rest-api-filters/).

`query.bibliographic` searches narrower bibliographic fields and `query.title` is available, but neither recreates the application's Boolean model. Crossref recommends cursor pagination and backing off after HTTP 429. See [Crossref REST API guidance](https://www.crossref.org/documentation/retrieve-metadata/rest-api/tips-for-using-the-crossref-rest-api/).

### Empirical observation

The first two current physical queries, limited to 20 journal articles each, reported:

```text
Query 1: 5,573,162 total results
Query 2: 2,559,567 total results
```

Returned records frequently lacked one or two intended concepts. Examples included:

- `Lean consumption` — 0/3 canonical evidence groups
- `Lean Six Sigma in the Energy Service Sector` — 0/3 exact groups
- lean-burn engine energy-efficiency work without Lean Management or manufacturing context
- Lean manufacturing articles with no energy concept
- energy-consumption manufacturing articles with no Lean concept

The strings are therefore not interpreted as exact Lean phrase AND exact Energy phrase AND exact Manufacturing phrase. Generic terms such as `management`, `manufacturing`, `production`, and `energy` create very large candidate universes.

## 9. Abstract availability

For `SearchField.ANY`, validation searches title plus abstract. It does not search container title, publisher, Crossref subjects, affiliation, or Crossref relevance score.

When the abstract is absent:

- A title term that matches is `MATCH`.
- A title term that does not match is `INDETERMINATE`.
- It is not `NON_MATCH`, because the missing abstract might contain the term.

For both medical examples:

```text
Crossref title + missing abstract → INDETERMINATE
Title + available external abstract → NON_MATCH
```

Complete abstracts would reject these examples. Their survival is predominantly the interaction of missing Crossref abstracts and recall-first uncertainty retention.

## 10. Validator defect assessment

No defect was found in Boolean or phrase evaluation:

- `AND` is not implemented as `OR`.
- Missing groups are not treated as `MATCH`.
- Bare `management` does not satisfy the exact Lean/Energy phrases.
- No substring or stemming collision was found.
- Exact phrases require contiguous normalized tokens.
- `NOT` follows three-valued inversion.
- Local constraints run after canonical validation.

The important behavior is intentional:

```text
missing abstract + unproven term → INDETERMINATE
INDETERMINATE → retained for recall
```

An existing test explicitly asserts that an unrelated title with no abstract is `INDETERMINATE`. Another Crossref fixture assumes that including all three anchors prevents medical `management` noise; real Crossref semantics invalidate that mock assumption.

## 11. UI counter semantics

### `Znaleziono w providerach`

This displays backend `result.total_count`. It is not the size of a canonical Boolean candidate set. The multi-query Crossref provider deliberately returns `total_count=None`, because overlapping physical-query totals cannot be summed. The exact origin of the observed approximately 2,564,124 value cannot be reconstructed; empirical query 2 reported 2,559,567, which is close but not proof of provenance.

### `Łącznie pobrano`

During an active job, `fetched_total` is the number of unique successfully mapped records admitted to canonical validation, not raw Crossref count.

### `Po lokalnych filtrach`

During an active in-memory job, `kept_total` means:

```text
MATCH or INDETERMINATE
AND runtime year/language/type/OA constraints pass
```

It does not mean canonical `MATCH`.

### Counter reconstruction defect

When progress is reconstructed from checkpoints, `kept_total` is populated from `canonical_accepted_count`, excluding retained `INDETERMINATE` records. The checkpoint does not persist `kept_count`.

Thus the label changes meaning:

- Active: actual retained MATCH + INDETERMINATE passing constraints.
- Reconstructed: MATCH only.

This is a separate reporting defect and makes retrospective interpretation of the observed ratio uncertain.

## 12. Root causes

### A. Crossref free-text semantics — CRITICAL

The API does not execute the intended conjunction; it requires only a fraction of words and ranks by relevance. Contribution to poor precision: HIGH.

### B. Physical query construction assumes unsupported semantics — CRITICAL

Quotes and whitespace are used as if they form exact multi-group anchors. Crossref does not guarantee that behavior. Contribution: HIGH.

### C. Missing Crossref abstracts — CRITICAL

25/40 sampled observations lacked abstracts, including both medical examples. Contribution: HIGH.

### D. Recall-first INDETERMINATE retention — CRITICAL

All missing-abstract sample observations became `INDETERMINATE`; even 0/3 evidence can be retained. Precision contribution: HIGH; recall protection: HIGH and intentional.

### E. Bounded-plan axis collapse — HIGH

Only `Manufacturing` is selected from the four-term third group. False-positive contribution: LOW; recall risk: HIGH.

### F. Limited validation metadata — MEDIUM

The validator uses title and abstract but not score, container, or subjects; external abstract lookup is disabled in the live pipeline. Contribution: MEDIUM.

### G. Insufficient per-record provenance — MEDIUM

Exact subquery, result rank, score, evidence, and validation result are not persisted per record. Diagnostic/reproducibility impact: HIGH.

### H. UI/reporting ambiguity — MEDIUM

The UI does not distinguish MATCH from INDETERMINATE, and checkpoint reconstruction changes `kept_total` semantics. Retrieval contribution: none; interpretation impact: HIGH.

## 13. Methodological assessment

### Precision risk — CRITICAL

Crossref retrieval is extremely broad, and missing abstracts turn many weak candidates into retained uncertainty. An 88% survival rate does not imply 88% topical relevance.

### Recall risk — HIGH

Recall-first retention protects retrieved uncertainty but cannot recover candidates never returned. The plan covers 3.57% of combinations and accidentally uses only one of four final-group anchors.

### Reproducibility — PARTIAL

The canonical query, aggregate plan, bound, coverage, provider/job/run, counters, and checkpoints are auditable. Exact per-record subquery, score/rank, evidence, and classification reason are not.

### Screening burden — HIGH

A 4,500-record increment left 3,969 records in the retained set. Repetition across million-record relevance universes creates a substantial unnecessary screening burden.

## 14. Findings

### BLOCKER — 0

No safety or repository blocker was found.

### MAJOR — 5

1. Crossref physical queries do not enforce canonical conjunction, producing million-record weak-match universes.
2. Missing abstracts plus recall-first policy retain zero-evidence records.
3. Bounded planner sampling collapses the third concept axis.
4. Per-record Crossref retrieval provenance is insufficient to trace false positives.
5. Checkpoint-reconstructed `kept_total` has different semantics from active progress.

### MINOR — 1

1. The existing provider mock assumes unrealistically strict Crossref anchor behavior and therefore misses the real failure mode.

### NOTE — 3

1. The real 4,500-record run is absent from the local database.
2. The empirical sample contains 39 unique records from two top-ranked queries and is not a population estimate.
3. Crossref returned HTTP 429 on the third request; testing stopped and no large fetch was performed.

## 15. Solution options

| Option | Mechanism | Precision | Recall | Risk | Complexity | Testability | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Per-record diagnostic provenance | Persist subquery, score/rank, abstract presence, validation and evidence | None immediately | None | Low | Medium | High | YES, first |
| Fielded/staged retrieval | Test `query.bibliographic`, `query.title`, field combinations, ranking and date shards | Likely moderate improvement | Unknown, potentially reduced | Medium–High | Medium | High | INVESTIGATE |
| Two-tier uncertainty handling | Put weak INDETERMINATE records in an explicit reviewable uncertainty tier | High improvement in main queue | Preserved only if uncertainty remains accessible | Medium | Medium | High | YES after measurement |
| Balanced bounded planner | Stratified/covering-array selection plus per-axis coverage metadata | Neutral | Improves bounded-plan recall | Low | Low–Medium | High | YES, separate WP |

A hard evidence gate that silently deletes 0/3 or 1/3 records is not recommended without a labeled recall study.

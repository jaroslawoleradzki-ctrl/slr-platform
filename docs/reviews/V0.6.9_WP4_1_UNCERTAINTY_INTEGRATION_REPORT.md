## Independent review findings addressed

The population-equivalence finding was reproduced: original WP4 sent every canonical MATCH or INDETERMINATE to MAIN, while Fetch All also applies execution constraints. WP4.1 adds explicit `execution_eligible`, preserving canonical status and tiers. The replay finding is now addressed with a dedicated adapter that consumes the actual WP2.1 path-level schema and produces candidate-level experimental inputs.

## Population-equivalence defect reproduction

Focused tests construct canonical MATCH and INDETERMINATE candidates with `execution_eligible=False`. Under every policy they are `EXECUTION_INELIGIBLE`, never MAIN, never relabeled NON_MATCH, and never counted as silently discarded uncertainty. Eligible candidates retain the original policy behavior.

## Corrected population contract

The model separates canonical status, execution eligibility, and analytical tier/policy. Eligible MATCH enters MAIN. Eligible INDETERMINATE enters MAIN for CURRENT_RECALL_FIRST and the appropriate uncertainty population for separation scenarios. Canonical NON_MATCH enters REJECTED. MATCH and INDETERMINATE rejected by execution constraints enter `EXECUTION_INELIGIBLE`.

## Execution-constraint semantics

The adapter maps actual WP2.1 `RetentionOutcome`: RETAINED is eligible; REJECTED_CONSTRAINTS is execution-ineligible; REJECTED_CANONICAL requires canonical NON_MATCH and remains canonical rejection. The adapter never reimplements Fetch All constraints or canonical validation.

## WP2 replay schema

WP2.1 `build_crossref_replay_dataset` emits one row per retrieval path. Actual durable candidate identity is `(job_id, search_run_id, source_record_id)` in the Crossref-only replay contract. Candidate facts are `canonical_status`, nested `canonical_group_evidence`, `metadata_completeness`, `retention_outcome`, DOI, and title. Query, index, cursor, rank, and score are path facts.

## Path-level to candidate-level adapter

`app/services/uncertainty_replay_adapter.py` groups actual replay rows by the WP2.1 identity. It returns one `WP2ReplayCandidate` per source candidate and preserves `retrieval_path_count` for diagnostics only. Candidate facts must agree across all paths; contradiction raises deterministic `WP2ReplayDataIntegrityError` rather than selecting a path arbitrarily.

## Candidate identity and deduplication

A two-path MATCH fixture produces two replay rows and exactly one WP4 candidate with `retrieval_path_count=2`. The integrated reconciliation fixture has six replay rows for five durable source candidates and produces five experiment candidates. Status and every experimental population count use candidate cardinality, never path cardinality.

## Field mapping

The adapter maps WP2.1 source identity to a stable WP4 candidate ID, canonical status directly, positive-group count from recorded group evidence, evidenced-group count from recorded MATCH group statuses, candidate-level missing fields from evidence, and abstract absence from provider metadata completeness. It carries completeness and retention outcome in the adapter result. It does not rerun the validator.

## Multi-path reconciliation

Every path row must agree on canonical status, exact canonical group evidence, metadata completeness, retention outcome, title, and DOI. Retrieval-specific fields may differ. Invalid schema, duplicate group indices, incompatible outcome/status pairs, or contradictory candidate facts raise an explicit deterministic data-integrity error.

## Positive-group / NOT semantics

The adapter consumes WP2.1 evidence and does not re-interpret the Boolean tree. A real WP2.1 `AND(Alpha, Gamma, NOT(Beta))` evidence fixture yields two positive groups and the adapted candidate reports two. A pure NOT query yields empty WP2.1 evidence and is adapted as a zero-positive-group MATCH without inventing a group.

## Missing-abstract / zero-evidence regression

A retained INDETERMINATE with absent provider abstract and zero evidenced groups adapts to `UNCERTAIN_NO_EVIDENCE`. It enters MAIN for CURRENT_RECALL_FIRST and UNCERTAINTY for SEPARATE_ALL_UNCERTAIN and EVIDENCE_TIERED_UNCERTAIN. Every built-in policy reports zero silently discarded uncertain candidates.

## Experimental policy safety

No built-in policy rejects an eligible INDETERMINATE. Ineligible candidates are explicitly separated from canonical rejection. STRONG, WEAK, and NO_EVIDENCE remain analytical evidence tiers, not relevance probabilities, recall estimates, precision estimates, or validated topical-relevance classes.
